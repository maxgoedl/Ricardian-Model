Preferences (both countries)

U = CμC WμW. The weights must sum to 1: editing one sets the other. Each is also the share of income spent on that good.

Wine is the numeraire: PW = 1, so the relative price p = PC/PW is the price of cheese in units of wine, and incomes and wages are measured in wine.
